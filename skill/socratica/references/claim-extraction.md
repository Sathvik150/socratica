# Claim extraction

A claim is an **atomic, checkable statement of fact**, copied from the text as written.

Keep:
- sentences with a concrete fact: a number, date, name, place, or a definite relation
- each half of a compound sentence as a separate claim ("X is 4,102 m tall, and it was first climbed in 1931" → two claims)

Skip:
- opinions and evaluations ("the best", "beautiful", "I think")
- instructions and advice ("Try…", "You should…")
- questions, greetings, filler ("Sure! Here is…", "Let me know…")
- hedged text ("might", "probably", "reportedly", "it is believed")
- code and command output

For a claim that starts with a pronoun ("It opened in 1991."), keep the previous sentence as
context so you know what "it" refers to when searching and judging.
